# Chandrakesh & Ankita | Wedding Invitation

Interactive digital wedding invitation website inspired by the sacred city of Varanasi and the divine blessings of Lord Shiva.

## Highlights
- Full-screen royal crest intro animation (`logo.png.png`)
- Varanasi golden-hour hero section for **Chandrakesh & Ankita** (Sengar Family)
- **Wedding Celebrations** schedule (`Ramayan Path`, `Haldi Hues`, `Mehendi`, `Wedding Vows`, `Pritibhoj`)
- **Our Moments** scroll-animated photo gallery
- **Our Venues** section with interactive Google Maps links and scannable QR codes for **Vrindavan Marriage Hall** and **Home Place**
- Frosted glassmorphism **RSVP** (`Chandrakesh Singh`) & live **Countdown** (`11 · December · 2026`)
- Cinematic **Auto Scroll** & Back-to-Top floating controls

## Getting Started
Open `index.html` in your browser or serve locally:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.
